What we are building

Structured freight matching for a fragmented market.

Route visibility

Open loads across key lanes, with route, cargo, truck type, and rate visible before the call starts.

AI parsing for WhatsApp

Turn unstructured load posts into clean listings with route, cargo, pricing, and pickup details. Proposed — not implemented in this prototype.

Regional expansion

Start with Kenya and scale to broader African corridors once the core network is working well.
